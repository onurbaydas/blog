I'm Onur Baydaş, a systems and infrastructure engineer based in Turkey. Since 2024
I've been designing, running, and troubleshooting on-premises infrastructure: the
kind of environments where there is no managed cloud service to fall back on and
every layer, from storage to scheduling, is your responsibility.

## What I work on

- **Virtualization and storage:** Proxmox VE clusters with Ceph, VMware vCenter/ESXi,
  hyperconverged platforms, TrueNAS/ZFS.
- **GPU infrastructure:** NVIDIA GPU servers for AI and research workloads, GPU
  partitioning with MIG and vGPU, job scheduling with Slurm.
- **Multi-user platforms:** I built a reservation and access platform for a shared
  GPU lab, covering user isolation, container access control, and day-to-day
  operations.
- **Container security:** Restricting Docker on shared machines, authorization
  policies with OPA/Rego, and the gaps those policies can't close.
- **Identity and operations:** Samba-based Active Directory, Kerberos/NFS home
  directories, centralized logging, and backup monitoring.

## What I write about

I write about problems I actually hit in production: the silent failures, the
misleading documentation, and what the source code says when the docs don't.
Every post is based on a real incident, tested on a live system, and backed by
links to the source.

## Writing work

I'm open to freelance technical writing on infrastructure, Linux, virtualization,
container security, and GPU/AI infrastructure topics.
