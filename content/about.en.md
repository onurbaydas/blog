I'm Onur Baydaş, a systems and infrastructure engineer based in Turkey. Since 2024
I've been designing, deploying, running, and troubleshooting on-premises
infrastructure: the kind of environments where there is no managed cloud service to
fall back on and every layer, from hardware and storage to identity and scheduling,
is your responsibility.

## Currently

- Scheduling, partitioning, and isolation on multi-user GPU infrastructure
- Operating virtualization and software-defined storage clusters
- Backup, monitoring, and high availability
- Technical writing in Turkish and English, drawn from production experience

## What I work on

- **Virtualization and storage:** Clustered virtualization, software-defined
  storage, hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI), enterprise NAS and ZFS-based storage.
- **GPU infrastructure:** GPU servers and clusters for AI and research workloads,
  GPU partitioning and virtual GPUs, HPC job scheduling, stress testing, and
  driver/framework compatibility.
- **Multi-user platforms:** Shared Linux workstations: user isolation, resource
  management, container access control, and day-to-day operations.
- **Container security:** Restricting the container runtime on shared machines,
  policy-as-code authorization, and cross-user isolation gaps.
- **Identity and access:** Directory services and identity management, Kerberos
  authentication, network home directories, PAM integration.
- **Backup and DR:** Hyperconverged backup, deduplication backup appliances, backup
  monitoring and reporting.
- **High availability:** Cluster resource management, block-level replication,
  active/passive service failover.
- **Observability:** Metrics, centralized logging, alerting, and dashboards;
  centralized syslog collection.
- **Hardware and lifecycle:** Rack server deployment, out-of-band management (BMC),
  RAID controller and disk failure analysis, vendor support processes.
- **Network and security:** Next-generation firewall management, segmentation,
  management network design.

## What I write about

I write about problems I actually hit in production: the silent failures, the
misleading documentation, and what the source code says when the docs don't.
Every post is based on a real incident, tested on a live system, and backed by
links to the source.
