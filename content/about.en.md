I'm Onur Baydaş, a systems and infrastructure engineer based in Turkey. Since 2024
I've been designing, running, and troubleshooting on-premises infrastructure: the
kind of environments where there is no managed cloud service to fall back on and
every layer, from storage to scheduling, is your responsibility.

## What I work on

- **Virtualization and storage:** Clustered virtualization, software-defined
  storage, hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI), enterprise NAS and ZFS-based storage.
- **GPU infrastructure:** GPU servers for AI and research workloads, GPU
  partitioning and virtual GPUs, HPC job scheduling.
- **Multi-user platforms:** Shared Linux systems for many users: user isolation,
  container access control, and day-to-day operations.
- **Container security:** Restricting the container runtime on shared machines,
  policy-as-code authorization, and the gaps those policies can't close.
- **Identity and operations:** Directory services and identity management, Kerberos
  authentication, network home directories, centralized logging, and backup monitoring.

## What I write about

I write about problems I actually hit in production: the silent failures, the
misleading documentation, and what the source code says when the docs don't.
Every post is based on a real incident, tested on a live system, and backed by
links to the source.

## Writing work

I'm open to freelance technical writing on infrastructure, Linux, virtualization,
container security, and GPU/AI infrastructure topics.
